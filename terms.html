Terms & Policies

Please read these practical service terms before placing an order or submitting a custom tailoring request. They explain our current process; they do not replace any rights provided by applicable consumer law.

1. Website orders

Submitting an order records your request with the store. It is not a final confirmation of stock, delivery date or payment until our team reviews the details and confirms them with you.

2. Custom tailoring and measurements

Custom requests are reviewed before a quotation, fabric availability and completion timeline are confirmed. Measurements supplied by customers are guidance for the tailor and may be checked or re-taken before work begins. If you are unsure, request assistance rather than guessing. Tell us promptly if you notice an error.

3. Quotation and payment

For custom tailoring, the current arrangement is a 30% deposit and 70% balance after the quotation is agreed. Do not send a deposit until the team confirms the quotation and payment instructions. Payments are coordinated manually; the website does not currently process M-Pesa or card payments.

4. Changes, cancellations and refunds

Changes after work has started may affect cost and timing. Contact the team as soon as possible. Cancellation requests are reviewed individually. Approval of a cancellation request does not itself confirm that a refund or deposit return is due; any applicable amount and process will be communicated separately, subject to applicable law.

5. Delivery and collection

Delivery availability, fees and estimated timing are confirmed individually based on destination and order. Please provide accurate contact and delivery details and remain available for coordination.

6. Privacy and reference images

Account and tailoring details are used to process your enquiry, coordinate your order and provide customer support. Private tailoring reference images are intended for the store team handling your request. Do not upload images or information you do not have permission to share.

7. Contact and corrections

If you submit incorrect details, use the edit option in your account while the request remains eligible, or contact the team promptly. Please quote your order or tailoring reference when seeking help.
