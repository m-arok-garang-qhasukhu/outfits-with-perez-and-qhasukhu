A LITTLE HELP

Frequently asked questions.

How does custom tailoring work?

Sign in, submit the tailoring request form, add your measurements or ask for guidance, and our team will review the request and confirm the quotation and timing.

What deposit is required?

Custom tailoring uses a 30% deposit and 70% balance after the quotation is agreed. Payment instructions are coordinated directly; submitting a request does not mean payment is due immediately.

Can I track my order?

Sign in to My Account to see orders linked to your account and their latest progress.

Do you deliver outside Kenya?

We welcome enquiries across East Africa. Delivery coverage, costs and timing are confirmed individually before accepting an order.

What if my measurements are incorrect?

Contact us promptly through WhatsApp so the team can review the garment stage and discuss available options. Changes may affect timing or cost.

How do I pay?

Website orders are recorded online. Our team currently coordinates payment instructions with customers through WhatsApp; automated M-Pesa checkout is not yet available.

WELCOME TO P&Q

A quick guide to the website

  1. Shop: Browse collections, use categories or search, open Quick View, choose a size and add an item to your bag.
  2. Place an order: Open your bag, sign in or create an account, enter delivery details and accept the Terms & Policies before submitting.
  3. Custom tailoring: Open My Account, sign in, complete the tailoring form, use the guided body measurements or ask our tailor for help, then submit.
  4. Track progress: Return to My Account to view your orders, tailoring progress, store messages and eligible request-edit options.
  5. Need help? Use FAQs or contact the team on WhatsApp. Payment instructions and delivery arrangements are confirmed directly.
